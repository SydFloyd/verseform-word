# Verseform for Word — Tight Roadmap

Each item is one user outcome with behavior, failure handling, and proof. Status is `done`, `next`, `queued`, or `blocked`.

| ID | Status | Outcome | Exit proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| `VFW-000` | done | Seed one coherent project: name it, define product/trust contracts, choose the cross-host Office.js shape, add a production-shaped XML manifest and capability task pane, and record the Word annotation feasibility gate and owner decisions. | Authorities agree; the scaffold builds and tests on Node 22; the manifest validates; no Bible, credentials, telemetry, or unproved Word behavior is present. |
| `VFW-010` | done | Prove the Word on the web walking interaction using native annotations. Detect one English reference locally after a delimiter, annotate the exact range, activate it by click or `Alt+Down`, show a fake passage preview in the task pane, explicitly replace only an observably fresh exact occurrence with passage and citation, and Undo to the reference. | Forty-one pure/fake-host tests prove local delimiter, mapping, observable stale refusal, lifecycle refusal, exact-ID startup recovery, same-runtime Undo resurrection repair, locked cross-pane ownership, explicit prior-ID post-close refusal, unsupported/readiness copy, and color/accessibility structure without network. Real Word on the web proved paragraph events, UTF-16 offsets, duplicate identity and replacement, one-step Undo, same-runtime repair, close/reopen-before-Undo, unsupported-host display, simultaneous cross-document operation, pane close/reopen recovery, browser lock/storage, and readable dark rendering. The final post-close Undo activated a current safe marker; the alternate prior-ID refusal is deterministic proof. `D-014` makes unreliable hover a best-effort enhancement; screen-reader and Windows forced-colors remain `VFW-040` release-host validation. `D-013` discloses the non-atomic WordApi 1.7 coauthor timing limitation while every observable stale case retains final exact recheck and fails closed. |
| `VFW-020` | next | Connect authorized DBS translations without adding document-data authority. Load the bounded catalog, prefer saved/NASB selection, fetch and normalize passages on demand, insert editable citation/attribution, and fail clearly offline. | Provider contracts and recorded payloads prove bounds and text normalization; a browser network assertion exposes only allowlisted catalog/chapter coordinates; real Word proves preview, insertion, stale response rejection, translation changes, and provider failure without losing prose. |
| `VFW-030` | queued | Harden reference intelligence and decide multilingual breadth. ## Scope gates

- VFW-010 closed the host feasibility gate with click and keyboard activation; hover remains best effort under `D-014`.
- VFW-020 may persist authorized provider responses only after cache age, byte, entry, schema, clear, and removal bounds are executable and no document prose enters the cache.
- VFW-030 cannot claim a language without a language-specific corpus and Word offset proof.
- VFW-040 cannot publish under DBS until DBS explicitly accepts that role and `D-011` resolves production hosting, support, and publisher identity.
